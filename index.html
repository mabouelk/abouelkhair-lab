How the lab works

Questions begin with mechanism and move through profiling, integration, and interpretation to produce research that is both rigorous and usable.

  • Design experiments that bridge immune function and molecular measurements.
  • Integrate orthogonal datasets instead of treating each assay in isolation.
  • Keep translation in view when choosing models, outputs, and collaborators.
What the lab studies

Three scientific pillars, one connected research language.

Each pillar is designed to stand alone when needed, but the strength of the program comes from the way immunology, virology, and system-scale analysis inform one another.
