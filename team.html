Principal Investigator & Lab Members

Abouelkhair Laboratory is led by Dr. Mohamed Abouelkhair and brings together graduate students, visiting scholars, and postdoctoral researchers working across AI-driven diagnostics, cancer biology, and One Health.

Lab snapshot

  • Comparative oncology and immunotherapy in aggressive companion animal cancers.
  • AI-assisted OMICs analysis for biomarker discovery and translational interpretation.
  • One Health pathogen identification across animal, environmental, and comparative datasets.

Principal Investigator

Dr. Mohamed Abouelkhair

Dr. Abouelkhair leads a research program at the intersection of machine learning, OMICs, and translational cancer biology. He is Associate Professor of Virology and Clinical Immunology in the Department of Veterinary Biomedical Sciences at the Shreiber School of Veterinary Medicine of Rowan University, and Associate Professor in the School of Translational Biomedical Engineering & Sciences.

  • Diplomate, American College of Veterinary Microbiologists (Immunology & Virology)
  • 2024 ASM Early Career Outstanding Researcher
  • 2024–2026 ASM Future Leaders Mentorship Fellow
