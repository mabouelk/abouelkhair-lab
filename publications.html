Selected recent work

Highlighted papers spanning cancer biology, AI-driven diagnostics, pathogen genomics, and comparative immunology. Full list at Google Scholar.

    Machine learning-based biomarker and therapeutic candidate discovery in companion animal cancers

    Research applying AI and OMICs to identify diagnostic biomarkers and immunotherapy targets in osteosarcoma and mammary carcinoma, with translational relevance to human oncology. View on Scholar →

  2. One Health

    AI-assisted metagenomic characterization of microbial communities in animal hosts and environmental samples

    Shotgun metagenomic and bioinformatics approaches linking microbial profiles to disease states and environmental conditions, including Yellowstone National Park ecosystems. View on Scholar →

  3. Vaccine

    Patented vaccine composition targeting multidrug-resistant Staphylococcus pseudintermedius

    Identification of key antigens and CRISPR-based characterization of virulence factors leading to a patented protective vaccine composition. View on Scholar →
